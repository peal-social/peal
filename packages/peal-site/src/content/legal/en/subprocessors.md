Last updated: 23 November 2026

Peal SAS ("we") runs the hosted Peal service with the providers below. Each one processes personal data only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement (GDPR Article 28). Some of that data is ours to answer for, as the [privacy policy](/privacy/) describes. The rest belongs to the organisations that use Peal, and we process it for them under the [data processing agreement](/dpa/).

A self-hosted instance of Peal uses none of these providers unless whoever runs it chooses them.

Anthropic receives data only when a member uses the writing assistant on Peal's own models. Crisp receives data only once someone opens the support chat. Turnstile runs only on sign-up, password reset and review links.

## Who is not on this list

The platforms you publish to, the ad networks an organisation connects, and Google when you sign in with it act as controllers under their own terms. So does a text, image or video provider an organisation brings with its own key: it acts for that organisation. The [privacy policy](/privacy/#recipients-and-sub-processors) says what each of them receives.

## Changes to this list

We announce a new sub-processor on this page and by email to the owners of each organisation at least 30 days before it starts processing their data. The [data processing agreement](/dpa/#sub-processors) says how an organisation can object, and what happens if we cannot meet the objection.

Write to privacy@peal.social for a copy of the safeguards that cover a transfer.
