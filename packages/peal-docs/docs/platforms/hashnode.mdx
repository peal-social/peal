How to connect a publication on Hashnode, and what an article there may hold. Hashnode takes [articles](/guides/channels/websites), not posts.

<Callout type="warning" title="Hashnode's Pro plan">
  Hashnode answers its API only for a publication on its Pro plan, paid on
  Hashnode. Without it, connecting the publication is refused, and an article
  sent there fails, each saying so.
</Callout>

## At a glance [#at-a-glance]

|             |                                                          |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Body        | Written as Markdown by Peal, with its images             |
| Tags        | 15 at most                                               |
| Categories  | None                                                     |
| On the site | Published at once, or a draft there; not scheduled there |
| Unpublish   | Removes the post, or the draft, from the publication     |

## Connect [#connect]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a personal access token">
    On Hashnode, open your account's settings, then **Developer**, and generate
    a personal access token for Peal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it in Peal">
    In **Channels**, under **Hashnode**, paste the token and give the
    publication's address, such as `blog.example.com` or `name.hashnode.dev`,
    then **Connect Hashnode**. With one publication, the address may be left
    empty.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Peal checks the token and the publication on Hashnode, then keeps the token encrypted: it is never shown again. The channel is the publication: yours, or a team's where you are an owner or an editor (a contributor only submits drafts for review there, and is refused). A member the publication keeps private is not in its visible list: the publication connects, and the first article says whether they may publish there. Revoke the token on Hashnode to cut Peal off; the channel is then marked **Reconnect**.

## Articles [#articles]

- Peal writes the body as Markdown, where everything you typed stays text.
- Each image goes to Hashnode's image upload first, once: images up to 8 MB, never SVG.
- The excerpt is the post's subtitle, the featured image its cover, each tag named by its slug.
- Hashnode cannot publish a post at a time itself: give the article a time with **Published at once**, and Peal sends it then.
- When Hashnode's answer is lost, Peal looks among the publication's latest posts for one of the same title and body before anything else, and never sends it twice.

## On your own server [#self-hosting]

Hashnode needs no app on a self-hosted server: each member brings a token. See [DEV and Hashnode](/self-hosting/platforms#devto-hashnode).
