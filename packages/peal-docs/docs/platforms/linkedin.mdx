## Connect a profile or pages [#connect]

Under **LinkedIn**, the button is **Connect LinkedIn**. On a server set up for company pages, you'll see two buttons instead: **Connect your profile** and **Connect company pages**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Your profile">

Click **Connect LinkedIn** (or **Connect your profile**), sign in on LinkedIn and allow Peal. Peal connects your own LinkedIn profile, and posts as you. It asks LinkedIn for your name and picture, and for the right to publish posts.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Company pages">

Click **Connect company pages**. You sign in to a second LinkedIn app, and Peal lists the pages you can post to, where you're an administrator or a content administrator. Select one or several. Each one becomes a channel that posts as the page.

Peal asks LinkedIn to post as your pages, read their posts, list the pages you administer and read their statistics. It asks for nothing about your own profile.

A page keeps working as long as you keep your role on it. If you lose that role, or leave the company, reconnect the page with another administrator's sign-in.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Channels** offers LinkedIn, and company pages, once the Peal server is set up for each (see [On your own server](#self-hosting)).

### Stay connected [#expiry]

LinkedIn lets Peal post for 60 days, then asks you to sign in again. The channel shows the date, and the workspace's owners and admins get a notice a week before. Reconnect before then. If you're still signed in on LinkedIn, it doesn't ask you again.

A page's access lasts 60 days too, unless LinkedIn gave the server's app refresh tokens, which it keeps for approved partners. In that case Peal renews the access a week ahead, for a year after the sign-in.

## Options [#options]

The composer offers these options. **API name** is the option's name in the API.

| Option                                   | API name          | What it takes                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Who can see this post**                | `visibility`      | Anyone unless you choose otherwise (`public`); connections only (`connections`), for a profile; signed-in LinkedIn members (`loggedIn`) |
| **Keep others from resharing this post** | `disableReshare`  | Yes or no                                                                                                                               |
| **Pages to mention**                     | `mentions`        | Each page as `Name <urn:li:organization:number>`, separated by semicolons ([see below](#mentions))                                      |
| **Link to show as a card**               | `linkUrl`         | A link                                                                                                                                  |
| **Link title**                           | `linkTitle`       | Required with a link                                                                                                                    |
| **Link description**                     | `linkDescription` | With a link                                                                                                                             |
| **Document title**                       | `documentTitle`   | For a PDF                                                                                                                               |

### Mention a page [#mentions]

To mention a page, type `@` and the page's full name in the text, as LinkedIn shows it. Then list each page under **Pages to mention**, as `Name <urn:li:organization:number>`, separated by semicolons.

You can't mention people, only pages.

## Comments [#comments]

Peal reads the comments under the posts of LinkedIn company pages, and you can answer them from Peal. See [Inbox](/guides/engage/inbox#linkedin).

## On your own server [#self-hosting]

A self-hosted server offers LinkedIn once its app is registered, and company pages once their app is too: see [LinkedIn](/self-hosting/platforms#linkedin) and [LinkedIn company pages](/self-hosting/platforms#linkedin-pages). Until then, **Channels** doesn't offer them.
