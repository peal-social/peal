## Connect an account [#connect]

Peal reaches an Instagram account in one of two ways. When the server has both, **Instagram** offers them as two buttons.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Instagram Login">

**Connect with Instagram** is the default. Sign in to the professional account itself on Instagram, and allow Peal to read its profile, publish, and read its insights. With this way of connecting:

- The account needs no Facebook Page.
- Its access lasts 60 days, and Peal renews it by itself a week before it ends.
- You can decline insights. The account then posts, but without counts in **Analytics**.

      </Tab>

  <Tab title="Facebook Login">

Click **Connect through a Facebook Page** and sign in with Facebook, as for Pages. Then select the Instagram accounts linked to your Pages. With this way of connecting:

- An account posts with its Page's access, which doesn't expire, so it stays connected as long as you manage the Page.
- It suits an account run from a Page. It's also the way to what only Meta's Facebook Login API gives, such as tagging products, collaborators and hashtag search, which Peal doesn't use yet.

      </Tab>

  </Tabs>

Both ways publish images, reels and carousels the same way, and read the same counts. The channels list says which way each Instagram channel came through, and reconnecting it signs in that way again.

If you connect through Instagram Login an account the workspace already has through a Page, its channel moves over, with its posts and schedule. The workspace keeps one channel per account.

## Options [#options]

Peal sends each option only when you set it. Some apply only to some media: the composer shows them for those, and scheduling refuses them on another post. In the composer, these are a reel's settings and **Collaborators**. **API name** is the option's name in the API.

| Option                           | API name        | Values                                                              | Left out             |
| -------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| **Format**                       | `format`        | `story` (see [stories and reels](/guides/posts#formats)), or `feed` | A feed post          |
| **Also share to the feed**       | `shareToFeed`   | A reel: yes or no                                                   | As Instagram decides |
| **Cover image**                  | `coverMediaId`  | A reel: a JPEG image of the library, 8 MiB at most                  | The cover frame      |
| **Cover frame, in milliseconds** | `thumbOffsetMs` | A reel: its frame, in milliseconds from its start, within it        | The first frame      |
| **Audio name**                   | `audioName`     | A reel: one line, 100 characters at most                            | Instagram's name     |
| **Trial reel**                   | `trialReel`     | A reel: `manual` or `performance`                                   | Not a trial          |
| **Collaborators**                | `collaborators` | One to three Instagram usernames                                    | None                 |

### Reels and stories [#reels]

On Instagram, a reel is a feed post of one video. A story takes none of the options above.

A cover image wins over a cover frame: when you set both, Peal sends only the image. Instagram renames a reel's audio only once.

A trial reel is shown first to accounts that don't follow yours. It reaches your followers when you share it in the Instagram app (`manual`), or by itself when it does well (`performance`).

Instagram shows the alt text of a post's images, up to 1,000 characters, and none on a video.

### Collaborators [#collaborators]

Under **Collaborators**, you can name one to three other accounts as the post's co-authors.

<Callout type="warning" title="Each collaborator gets an invitation">
  Each collaborator is another account, which receives an invitation to be named
  as a co-author. Instagram doesn't document collaborators for accounts
  connected with Instagram Login: when Instagram refuses such a post, the
  channel shows **Failed** with Meta's own code.
</Callout>

## Comments [#comments]

Peal reads the comments under your published posts on Instagram, and you can answer them from Peal. See [Inbox](/guides/engage/inbox#meta).

You can read and answer the account's direct messages too. Meta takes an answer within 24 hours of the person's last message. Where the server allows it, a member can still answer by hand in Peal's web app for up to 7 days. See [Messenger and Instagram](/guides/engage/inbox#meta-messages).

Once the channel's direct messages are on, you can also send the author of a comment one [private reply](/guides/engage/inbox#private-replies), within seven days of the comment. A member sends it, or a [comment rule](/guides/engage/inbox#comment-rule-private-replies) does after its public reply.
