A post on Pinterest becomes a Pin on one of the account's boards. Peal creates no board, so make the one you need on Pinterest before you schedule.

## At a glance [#at-a-glance]

| ----------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Text              | {{platform-pinterest-text-en}}    |
| Images and videos | {{platform-pinterest-media-en}}   |

What each file type must be is in [Images and videos](/guides/media#platforms).

## Connect an account [#connect]

Under **Pinterest**, click **Connect Pinterest** and sign in on Pinterest. Then allow Peal to:

- See your boards and Pins.
- Create Pins.
- Read your account's name and picture.

Peal asks for nothing about secret boards, so it never lists them and can't pin to them.

**Channels** offers Pinterest once the Peal server is set up for it (see [On your own server](#self-hosting)).

## Options [#options]

The composer offers these options. **API name** is the option's name in the API.

| Option                     | API name         | In the composer                                                       |
| -------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Board**                  | `boardId`        | Required, one of the account's                                        |
| **Board section**          | `boardSectionId` | A section of the chosen board                                         |
| **Title**                  | `title`          | 100 characters at most                                                |
| **Link**                   | `link`           | Where a click on the Pin leads, starting with `http://` or `https://` |
| **Video cover, at second** | `coverSecond`    | The first frame unless you choose                                     |
| **Dominant colour**        | `dominantColor`  | Such as `#6E7874`                                                     |

A Pin has one alt text, the first file's. A carousel's images share the Pin's title, description and link.

### Boards [#boards]

On a Pinterest channel, the composer reads the account's boards from Pinterest, up to 500 of them. Once you've chosen one, it reads that board's sections too.

A board deleted since, or one beyond the 500 read, stays chosen and shows as not in the list. When Pinterest doesn't answer, click **Try again** to read the list again.

## Comments [#comments]

Peal doesn't read comments on Pinterest yet.

## On your own server [#self-hosting]

A self-hosted server offers Pinterest once its app is registered: see [Pinterest](/self-hosting/platforms#pinterest). Until then, **Channels** doesn't offer it.
