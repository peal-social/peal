Each Google Business Profile location you connect becomes a channel, and a post there can be an update, an event or an offer. Google takes posts only for locations it has verified.

## Connect locations [#connect]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Under **Google Business Profile**, click **Connect Google Business Profile**
    and sign in with Google. Then allow Peal to manage your Business Profile.
    Google has one permission for everything about a profile, and Peal uses it
    only to list your locations and make their posts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the locations">
    If your sign-in reaches one location, Peal connects it. If it reaches
    several, Peal lists them, each with its address, and you select those this
    workspace posts to. Each location is a channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only locations Google has verified are listed, because Google takes no post for the others. Verify the business in your Business Profile first, then connect again. One sign-in offers 200 locations at most.

<Callout
  type="warning"
  title="A location that changes hands stops taking posts"
>
  A channel keeps the account its location was reached through. If the location
  moves to another account or location group, or the person who connected it no
  longer manages it, its posts fail. Disconnect the channel, then connect the
  location again.
</Callout>

**Channels** offers Google Business Profile once the Peal server is set up for it (see [On your own server](#self-hosting)).

## Options [#options]

The composer offers these options. **API name** is the option's name in the API.

| Option                       | API name          | In the composer                                          |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Post type**                | `postType`        | An update unless you choose an event or an offer         |
| **Title**                    | `title`           | 58 characters at most; required for an event or an offer |
| **Starts**                   | `startsAt`        | Required for an event or an offer                        |
| **Ends**                     | `endsAt`          | Required for an event or an offer                        |
| **Button**                   | `callToAction`    | On an update or an event                                 |
| **Button link**              | `callToActionUrl` | On an update or an event                                 |
| **Coupon code**              | `couponCode`      | On an offer                                              |
| **Link to redeem the offer** | `redeemUrl`       | On an offer                                              |
| **Terms and conditions**     | `terms`           | On an offer                                              |

### How Google Business Profile treats them [#options-behaviour]

The post's text becomes the post's description on Google.

An event's or an offer's dates are on the location's own clock, whatever your time zone. The end has to come after the start.

Every button opens its link, which starts with `https://` or `http://`. The exception is **Call now**, which rings the number on the profile and takes no link. An offer shows Google's own button.

## Comments [#comments]

Peal doesn't read comments on Google Business Profile yet.

## On your own server [#self-hosting]

A self-hosted server offers Google Business Profile once its app is registered, with a Google project that Google gave access to its Business Profile APIs: see [Google Business Profile](/self-hosting/platforms#google-business). Until then, **Channels** doesn't offer it.
