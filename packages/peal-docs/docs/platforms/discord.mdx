## Connect [#connect]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a webhook">
    In Discord, open the channel's settings, then **Integrations**,
    **Webhooks**, and make a webhook for Peal. Its name is what the channel
    shows as the author of Peal's posts. Choose **Copy Webhook URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in Peal">
    On **Channels**, under **Discord**, paste the address into **Webhook
    address** and choose **Connect Discord**. Peal reads the webhook on Discord
    and keeps its address encrypted: it is never shown again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- The address starts with `https://discord.com/api/webhooks/`. Peal refuses any other before it calls anything.
- Only an incoming webhook, the kind a channel's settings make, posts messages.
- Another webhook of the same channel reconnects its channel with the new address.
- To cut Peal off, delete the webhook in the channel's settings. Peal then marks the channel to reconnect and calls it no more.

## What a post becomes [#posts]

- The text is Discord's markdown, as you type it: `**bold**`, `> quote`, a link. Peal changes nothing in it.
- A mention notifies nobody unless **Notify the people and roles it mentions** is on: `@everyone`, a role or a person typed in the text shows, and pings no one.
- The parts of a [thread](/guides/posts#threads) are messages after one another in the channel.
- In a forum or media channel, the first part opens a forum post titled with **Forum post title**, and the later parts go in it. Without a title, Discord refuses the post (`forumThreadNameRequired`); any other channel refuses a title.

## Options [#options]

No option is required, and each goes with every part but the title, which the first part takes.

| Option                                      | API name         | Values                           | Left out |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | -------------------------------- | -------- |
| **Notify the people and roles it mentions** | `mentions`       | Yes or no                        | No       |
| **Send without a notification**             | `silent`         | Yes or no                        | No       |
| **Show no preview of its links**            | `suppressEmbeds` | Yes or no                        | No       |
| **Forum post title**                        | `threadName`     | One line, 100 characters at most | None     |

## Edit and delete [#live]

Peal edits a post's text there and deletes it, every part of it: see [After a post is published](/guides/posts/published). An edit keeps the files.

## When an answer is lost [#lost]

Discord keeps no key to send a message again under, and lets a webhook list no message. When Discord takes a post and its answer never comes, the post is **To check**: look at the channel, then confirm it or send it again from its page. Peal never sends it again by itself, and sends no part after it.

## What Peal does not do there [#limits]

Peal reads nothing back from Discord: no counts, no replies, no inbox. It posts in no existing thread, and sends no embed, poll or button.
