Peal posts to Facebook Pages, as a feed post, a story or a reel. One sign-in with Facebook can connect several Pages, each as its own channel.

## Connect Pages [#connect]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Facebook">
    Under **Facebook Pages**, click **Connect Facebook Pages** and sign in with
    Facebook. Then allow Peal to list your Pages and post on them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Pages">
    Back in Peal, select the Pages the workspace posts to, then click
    **Connect**. Each one becomes a channel. You have 15 minutes to choose.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your sign-in reaches only one Page, Peal connects it straight away. Only Pages you can create content on are listed. Those the workspace already has are marked, and selecting one reconnects it.

If connecting fails before any Page is connected, Peal says why. You can choose again, or click **Sign in with Facebook again** for a new list. Once the choice has expired or been made, only signing in again brings the Pages back.

## Options [#options]

Peal sends each option only when you set it. Some apply only to some media: the composer shows them for those, and scheduling refuses them on another post. In the composer, a video has its **Video title**. **API name** is the option's name in the API.

| Option          | API name     | Values                                                                        | Left out    |
| --------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| **Format**      | `format`     | `story` or `reel` (see [stories and reels](/guides/posts#formats)), or `feed` | A feed post |
| **Video title** | `videoTitle` | A video or a reel: one line, 255 characters at most                           | No title    |

## Comments [#comments]

Peal reads the comments under your published posts on your Pages, and you can answer them from Peal. See [Inbox](/guides/engage/inbox#meta).
