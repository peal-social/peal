You connect a Telegram channel or group with a bot you make yourself, and Peal posts as that bot. It posts in channels and groups only, never in a private chat with a person.

## At a glance [#at-a-glance]

Here's what a Telegram post can hold:

|                     |                                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Text                | {{platform-telegram-text-en}}        |
| Text with media     | {{platform-telegram-text-media-en}}  |
| Images and videos   | {{platform-telegram-media-en}}       |
| Their size together | {{platform-telegram-media-total-en}} |
| Parts               | {{platform-telegram-parts-en}}       |
| Alt text            | {{platform-telegram-alt-en}}         |

What each file type must be is in [Images and videos](/guides/media#platforms). Telegram takes JPEG and PNG photos, MP4 videos, and a GIF, which has to go alone. Each Telegram chat counts as one account on your [plan](/guides/workspace/billing).

## Connect a chat [#connect]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a bot">
    In Telegram, open @BotFather, Telegram's own bot for making bots. Send
    `/newbot` and follow its steps. It answers with the bot's token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the bot to the chat">
    In a channel, add the bot as an administrator who can post messages. In a
    group, add it as a member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the token in Peal">
    On **Channels**, under **Telegram**, paste the token into **Bot token** and
    type the chat in **Chat**. Then click **Connect Telegram**. Peal checks that
    the bot can post there and keeps the token encrypted, so you won't see it
    again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In **Chat**, type the chat's public name, such as `@name`, or its id. A channel's or a supergroup's id starts with `-100`.

To cut Peal off, revoke the bot's token with @BotFather, or remove the bot from the chat. Peal then marks the channel to reconnect.

When a group becomes a supergroup, Telegram gives it a new id, and posts to it fail with `chatMigrated`. Connect the chat again as a new channel, then disconnect the old one. The old channel counts on your plan until you do.

## What a post becomes [#posts]

Peal sends plain text, with no bold, italics or other formatting. Telegram still makes links, `@names`, hashtags and `/commands` clickable. An `@name` in a group notifies that person, and Peal can't turn that off.

On its own, the text can be up to {{platform-telegram-text-en}}. With images or videos, it becomes their caption and can be up to {{platform-telegram-text-media-en}}. Peal checks the length when you schedule the post.

Several files go out as one album, with the text as the first file's caption. The parts of a [thread](/guides/posts#threads) are replies, each to the part before it.

## Options [#options]

None of these is required, and each one goes with every part. **API name** is the option's name in the API:

| Option                               | API name      | Values    | Left out |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------- | --------- | -------- |
| **Show a preview of its first link** | `linkPreview` | Yes or no | Yes      |
| **Send without sound**               | `silent`      | Yes or no | No       |

A part with images or videos never shows a link preview, whatever **Show a preview of its first link** says.

## Edit and delete [#live]

Peal can edit a post's text, or its caption, and delete the post with every message of an album (see [After a post is published](/guides/posts/published)). Telegram deletes a message only within 48 hours of sending it. After that, Peal doesn't ask Telegram and tells you so (`deletionWindowPassed`), and you delete the post in Telegram yourself.

## When an answer is lost [#lost]

Telegram has no key that lets Peal send a message again safely, and a bot can't read a chat's messages. So when Telegram takes a post and its answer never reaches Peal, the post shows **To check**. Look at the chat, then confirm the post or send it again from its page.

Peal never sends it again on its own, and it doesn't send the parts that come after it.

## What Peal doesn't do on Telegram [#limits]

Peal doesn't read anything back from Telegram, so there are no counts, replies or inbox for it. It doesn't post in a topic of a forum group, and it sends no formatting, buttons or documents.
