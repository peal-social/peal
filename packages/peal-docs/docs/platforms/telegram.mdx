How to connect a Telegram channel or group with a bot of your own, what a post there may hold, and the options it takes.

## At a glance [#at-a-glance]

|                   |                                     |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Text              | {{platform-telegram-text-en}}       |
| Text with media   | {{platform-telegram-text-media-en}} |
| Images and videos | {{platform-telegram-media-en}}      |
| Parts             | {{platform-telegram-parts-en}}      |
| Alt text          | {{platform-telegram-alt-en}}        |

What each file type must be is in [Images and videos](/guides/media#platforms): photos (JPEG, PNG), MP4 videos, and a GIF, which goes alone. A Telegram chat counts as one account on the [plan](/guides/workspace/billing).

## Connect [#connect]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a bot">
    In Telegram, open @BotFather, Telegram's own bot for making bots, send
    `/newbot`, and follow its steps. It answers with the bot's token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the bot to the chat">
    In a channel, add the bot as an administrator who may post messages. In a
    group, add it as a member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it in Peal">
    On **Channels**, under **Telegram**, paste the token into **Bot token**,
    give the chat in **Chat**, and choose **Connect Telegram**. Peal checks the
    bot may post there, and keeps the token encrypted: it is never shown again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- Name the chat by its public name, such as `@name`, or by its id: a channel's or a supergroup's starts with `-100`.
- Peal posts in channels and groups, never in a private chat with a person.
- To cut Peal off, revoke the bot's token with @BotFather, or remove the bot from the chat. Peal then marks the channel to reconnect.
- When a group becomes a supergroup, Telegram gives it a new id and posts fail (`chatMigrated`): connect the chat again, as a new channel.

## What a post becomes [#posts]

- The text is plain text: Telegram makes its links clickable, and nothing else is formatted.
- A text alone takes 4,096 characters. A text with images or videos is their caption, and takes 1,024: Peal checks it when the post is scheduled.
- Several files go as one album, the text the first one's caption.
- The parts of a [thread](/guides/posts#threads) are replies, each to the part before.

## Options [#options]

No option is required, and each goes with every part.

| Option                               | API name      | Values    | Left out |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------- | --------- | -------- |
| **Show a preview of its first link** | `linkPreview` | Yes or no | Yes      |
| **Send without sound**               | `silent`      | Yes or no | No       |

A part with images or videos shows no link preview, whatever it says.

## Edit and delete [#live]

Peal edits a post's text, or its caption, and deletes it, every message of an album: see [After a post is published](/guides/posts/published). Telegram deletes a message only within 48 hours of sending it.

## When an answer is lost [#lost]

Telegram keeps no key to send a message again under, and lets a bot read no chat's messages. When Telegram takes a post and its answer never comes, the post is **To check**: look at the chat, then confirm it or send it again from its page. Peal never sends it again by itself, and sends no part after it.

## What Peal does not do there [#limits]

Peal reads nothing back from Telegram: no counts, no replies, no inbox. It posts in no topic of a forum group, and sends no formatting, button or document.
