You'll find the assistant in the composer, where it drafts and reworks a post's text. It can also make an image for the post.

## Ask the assistant [#assistant]

The toolbar under the composer's text has an **Assistant** menu when the workspace has its own [text provider](/guides/workspace#providers), or when the Peal instance has an AI provider. The menu works on the text for all channels.

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose an action">

Open **Assistant** and click what it should do.

</Step>
<Step title="Read the suggestion">

The suggestion opens in a preview. Nothing in your post changes yet.

</Step>
<Step title="Use it or drop it">

Click **Use this text**, or **Add these hashtags**, which adds them on a line of their own. To drop the suggestion, click **Discard**.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="A suggestion can still be wrong">
  The assistant keeps mentions, hashtags and links, and it's told not to add
  facts. Read the suggestion before you use it.
</Callout>

### What each action does [#actions]

The menu has five actions:

| Action                | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Write a post…**     | Say what the post is about, in your own words, and the assistant writes a first text, in the subject's language.                                                                                                                        |
| **Rewrite in a tone** | Professional, friendly, casual, enthusiastic or witty. It keeps the meaning, the language and about the length.                                                                                                                         |
| **Shorten to fit**    | One of the platforms of the channels you picked. Before you pick any, each platform whose limit Peal knows. With images or videos attached, it shortens to the caption's limit where the platform has one (Telegram: 1,024 characters). |
| **Translate into**    | English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Japanese or Chinese (simplified).                                                                                                                                 |
| **Suggest hashtags**  | Up to 8 that the text doesn't have yet.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

### Write a post from a subject [#write]

**Write a post…** is offered whether the text is empty or not. If you use its text, it replaces the one you had.

You can choose a tone. When the post goes to a platform with a short limit (X, Bluesky, Threads or Mastodon), you can also choose the one whose limit the text must fit.

The assistant is told not to add facts, figures, names, dates or links that the subject doesn't give. It adds hashtags only when the subject asks for them. For another idea, ask again, and each try counts as one request.

## Make an image [#images]

**Choose from the library** offers **Generate an image** when the workspace has its own [image provider](/guides/workspace#providers), or when the instance has an image model. It makes an image from a description, puts it in the library and picks it for the post. See [Generate an image](/guides/media#generate).

An image from the instance's model costs 5 of the day's requests. One from the workspace's own provider costs none.

## Limits [#limits]

The assistant has these limits:

| Limit                                  | Value                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Text or subject                        | Up to 5,000 characters                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Requests a day                         | 100 per workspace unless the instance sets another figure, counted from midnight UTC. On Peal Cloud, the plan's, shared by the workspaces of the billing account. The menu shows how many are left today. |
| With the workspace's own text provider | None counts. The menu says the workspace's provider writes, and that provider takes up to 500 requests a day.                                                                                             |

A request that couldn't reach the AI provider doesn't count. One that took more than 90 seconds does, because the provider may have worked on it.

Requests are counted per workspace, never per person, so every member shares the workspace's day. On Peal Cloud, the workspaces of one billing account share one count, including those a [partner](/developers/embedding) owns.

## Where your text goes [#privacy]

Your text, or the subject, goes to the workspace's own text provider if it set one. Otherwise it goes to the AI provider the instance's administrator chose, and nowhere else. Peal saves neither the text you send nor the suggestion, and never writes them to its logs.
