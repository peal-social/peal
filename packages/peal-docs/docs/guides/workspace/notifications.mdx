Every notification shows in Peal, on the notifications page, and some also come by email. You choose which ones reach your inbox.

## What you hear about [#events]

Peal tells the members it concerns when something happens to a post, a review, a channel, a webhook, a boost or a comment. The tables below list each notification, when it comes and who gets it.

### Posts [#post-events]

| Notification                | When                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Who hears it                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A post went out             | The post is live on one of its channels.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | The post's author, and members who follow every post (see below)                                                                                                      |
| A post did not go out       | The post failed on one of its channels, for [one of the reasons below](#failure-reasons).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | The same                                                                                                                                                              |
| A post may have gone out    | Peal couldn't tell whether a channel published it. Check the platform before you post it again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | The same                                                                                                                                                              |
| A post stopped repeating    | A [rule that repeats a post](/guides/posts/queues#recycling) ended for a reason someone can fix or needs to know: its post was deleted, whoever started it can no longer schedule, none of its channels is left, a platform refuses what the next post would say, the month has no room in the plan, or its queue was deleted or has no free slot. You're told again each time it ends after being resumed.                                                                               | Whoever started the rule, and members who follow every post. If that person left, or a key started the rule, every member who may schedule posts                      |
| A feed stopped making posts | A [feed](/guides/posts/queues#feeds) stopped: its site says it's gone, it couldn't be read or gave something that isn't a feed for seven days, whoever added it can no longer make posts there, or its queue was deleted. You're told again each time it stops after being resumed.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Whoever added the feed, and members who follow every post. If that person left, or a key added the feed, every member who may schedule posts                          |
| Follow-ups did not go out   | Follow-ups of a [follow-up rule](/guides/posts/queues#follow-up-ends) failed, or may have gone out without Peal knowing (check the platform: Peal never sends one again), or their seven days passed while the platform put them off, their channel wasn't connected again, or the rule waited for an approval. At most one notification per rule and per day, with how many: those that end after it are told the next day, except those that may have gone out, which are told at once. | Whoever started the rule, and members who follow every post. If that person left, or a key started the rule, every member who may schedule posts                      |
| A follow-up rule stopped    | A [follow-up rule](/guides/posts/queues#follow-up-ends) ended by itself: whoever started it can no longer publish, or its channel was disconnected. Once per rule.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Whoever started the rule, and members who follow every post. If the person who started it can no longer publish, every member who may schedule posts, and that person |

A post's notifications go to two groups:

- its author, the member who created it, whatever their role;
- the members who may schedule posts (owners, admins and editors) and chose to hear about every post in their [preferences](#email).

A post created with an API key has no author, so its notifications go to every member who may schedule posts. A member who left the workspace hears nothing more about it. Their posts' notifications go to every member who may schedule posts, as for a post without an author.

Each notification about a post names one channel. A post on three channels can bring three notifications, and a post sent again brings new ones. A cancelled post brings none.

Once someone says whether a post to check went out, or sends it again, its notifications are marked read for everyone. Their emails that haven't gone yet are dropped.

### Review [#review-events]

| Notification                     | When                                                                                                          | Who hears it                                              |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| A post waits for an approval     | Someone asked for a post to go out, in a workspace that asks for [approvals](/guides/posts/review).           | Members who may approve posts: owners, admins and editors |
| A post was approved              | Someone approved it, in Peal or through a review link. If it was approved after its time, it's a draft again. | Its author, whoever asked, and whoever commented on it    |
| Changes were requested on a post | It's a draft again, with a comment that says what to change.                                                  | The same                                                  |
| A post was commented on          | A member, or a reviewer through a link, commented on it.                                                      | The same                                                  |

### Channels and webhooks [#channel-events]

| Notification                                | When                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Who hears it                                        |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| A channel must be reconnected               | The platform no longer accepts Peal's access. Nothing goes out on the channel until someone reconnects it.                                                                                                                                                                 | Members who may connect channels: owners and admins |
| A channel must be reconnected soon          | The channel's access can't be renewed, and ends on the date the notification gives.                                                                                                                                                                                        | The same                                            |
| A channel's direct messages were turned off | The account was connected again without allowing its direct messages, so Peal turned them off and deleted their conversations. To read them again, click **Read its messages** for the channel in **Settings** > **Direct messages**, then **Allow**, on Bluesky.          | The same                                            |
| A webhook endpoint was turned off           | Every delivery to a [webhook](/developers/webhooks/delivery#delivery) endpoint failed for 5 days, or its receiver answered `410 Gone`. Peal sends it nothing more until someone turns it back on.                                                                          | Members who may manage webhooks: owners and admins  |
| Webhook deliveries are failing              | A delivery to a [webhook](/developers/webhooks/delivery#delivery) endpoint that's still on failed for good, with every attempt used up. Send it again from the [delivery log](/developers/webhooks/delivery#log) once the receiver works. At most once a day per endpoint. | Members who may manage webhooks: owners and admins  |

### Why a post did not go out [#failure-reasons]

A notification about a failed post gives one of these reasons:

| Reason                                                                         | What to do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The platform refused its text or its format                                    | Check the text against the platform's rules, then schedule it in a new post.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Its channel was disconnected from the workspace before it went out             | [Connect](/guides/channels) the account again, then schedule it in a new post.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Its channel was turned off before it went out                                  | [Turn the channel back on](/guides/channels#turn-off), then schedule it in a new post.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| One of its images or videos was deleted from the media library                 | Schedule it in a new post, with media from the library.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| The platform refused one of its images or videos                               | Check the media against [what the platform accepts](/guides/media#platforms).                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Peal was unavailable at the scheduled time, and the post came too late to send | The server was down past the post's time. [Post it again](/guides/posts/schedule#follow-up), or schedule it in a new post.                                                                                                                                                     |
| Someone checked the platform and did not find it                               | [Post it again](/guides/posts/schedule#follow-up).                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| The platform kept turning requests away, and Peal stopped trying               | The platform limited how often Peal posts. [Post it again](/guides/posts/schedule#follow-up) later.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| The platform's daily allowance of uploads stayed used up                       | YouTube's daily uploads, for the Peal server or for the channel, ran out and stayed that way for a day after the post's time. [Post it again](/guides/posts/schedule#follow-up) after midnight Pacific Time.                                                                   |
| The account's authorization stopped working                                    | [Reconnect](/guides/channels#reconnect) the channel, then [post it again](/guides/posts/schedule#follow-up).                                                                                                                                                                   |
| The platform could not be reached                                              | Peal tried for over two hours. [Post it again](/guides/posts/schedule#follow-up) once the platform answers.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| The platform takes only private posts from this server's app                   | TikTok hasn't audited the server's app yet. Make the account private and the post visible to **Only me**, or ask the server's operator. See [TikTok](/platforms/tiktok#connect).                                                                                               |
| The platform refused this server's app until its operator pays for it          | Your post isn't at fault. X stopped taking posts from the server's app, whose credits ran out or reached their spending cap, and Peal kept trying for a day after the post's time. Once the server's operator has fixed it, [post it again](/guides/posts/schedule#follow-up). |

## The notifications page [#inbox]

A blue dot on your avatar, at the bottom left, shows that notifications wait unread, across all your workspaces, and the account menu gives their count. It checks every 30 seconds while the tab is open, and again as soon as you come back to the tab. Screen readers announce new ones when they arrive.

Click your avatar, then **Notifications**, or click **Notifications** under **Settings** in any workspace, to open **Notifications**, newest first. Each one says what happened, in which workspace and on which channel, and links to the post or to the workspace's channels. From there, you can:

- Click **Mark as read** on one notification, or **Mark all as read**.
- Click **Show more** to see older ones.

Read notifications stay in the list, and times are in your browser's time zone. You also see the notifications of workspaces you've left, under "a workspace you left".

## By email [#email]

From **Notifications**, click **Email preferences**, or click **Notification preferences** under **Settings** in any workspace. There you choose two things:

- which notifications also reach you by email;
- under **Tell me about**, whether you hear about every post of your workspaces, not only the ones you wrote. This is off until you turn it on.

Each choice is saved as you change it, and applies in all your workspaces. Here are the emails and where they start:

| Email me when                                                                           | Until you change it |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| A post did not go out                                                                   | On                  |
| A post may have gone out, and needs checking on the platform                            | On                  |
| A post stops repeating                                                                  | On                  |
| A feed stops making posts                                                               | On                  |
| A follow-up or comment reply rule stops by itself, or follow-ups do not go out          | On                  |
| A post waits for my approval, or a post I follow is commented on, approved or sent back | On                  |
| A channel must be reconnected, now or soon, or its direct messages were turned off      | On                  |
| Deliveries to a webhook endpoint fail for good, at most once a day for each endpoint    | On                  |
| A post went out                                                                         | Off                 |
| A published post received new comments                                                  | Off                 |
| A week starts: what went out last week and what is planned, each Monday                 | Off                 |

When a post didn't go out because its channel must be reconnected, members who may reconnect channels and get that email aren't also emailed about each post: the channel's email covers it. The post still shows in their notifications.

Some emails are always sent:

- A webhook endpoint turned off goes to the members who manage webhooks. The systems that listened on it hear nothing more until someone acts.
- What happens to a boost, and an ad network sign-in to renew, always come by email too, because an owner answers for what's spent.

Emails are written in your language. When the mail server turns an email away for now, Peal tries again for about five hours. An email waiting to go out is dropped if you leave the workspace, or turn that email off, in the meantime.

## The weekly digest [#digest]

Turn on **A week starts** in your email preferences to get a weekly digest. Each Monday from 06:19 UTC, Peal emails you the week that ended at midnight UTC and the one it begins, for every workspace whose posts you can read. For each workspace, it gives:

- how many posts went out, failed or need checking, and how many are scheduled or waiting for an approval;
- the first few posts of each week, by the start of their text;
- the engagement of the week's posts, where you can read analytics.

Days and times are in UTC. A workspace with nothing in those two weeks is left out, and if there's nothing anywhere, no email is sent. The digest holds the workspace's own posts only, never a comment or who wrote one.

To stop it, use your mail app's unsubscribe button, which turns off the digest and nothing else. You can also follow the link at its foot to your notification preferences in your workspace.

The digest goes once a week. When the mail server turns it away for now, Peal tries again in the next hours of the same Monday. A digest turned away for good isn't sent again until the next Monday.

## From the API [#api]

Your notifications and preferences belong to you, not to a workspace, so they live under `/api/me`:

| Endpoint                                           | What it does                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /api/me/notices`                              | Lists them, newest first, 20 a page                                                                                                            |
| `GET /api/me/notices/unread`                       | Counts the unread ones                                                                                                                         |
| `POST /api/me/notices/read`                        | Marks up to 100 of them read                                                                                                                   |
| `POST /api/me/notices/read-all`                    | Marks them all                                                                                                                                 |
| `GET` and `PATCH /api/me/notification-preferences` | Read and change which ones are emailed, `email.weeklyDigest`, the [weekly digest](#digest), and `everyPost`, whether you hear about every post |

Each notification is data, such as a post's id, a channel and a reason, for the client to write in its reader's language.

These endpoints take a session, or an OAuth token that isn't bound to one workspace, such as the command line's. API keys, which belong to a workspace, get 403, and so do MCP clients, which act in one workspace. The [API reference](/reference) lists their parameters.
