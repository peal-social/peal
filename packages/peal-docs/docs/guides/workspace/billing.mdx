On Peal Cloud, each workspace is on its [billing account](#accounts)'s plan. That's the free tier, or per-account pricing once you add a payment method.

Both include every platform, the calendar, analytics, the API, the [MCP server](/developers/mcp) and the [command line](/developers/cli), with unlimited members, workspaces and posts. A server you run yourself bills nothing and limits nothing ([Self-hosting](/self-hosting/options#billing)).

## Per-account pricing [#pricing]

A billing account pays for the social media accounts connected across all its workspaces, each month. The month's accounts, counted by the day as below, are priced band by band on one graduated ladder. Each price is shown excluding VAT and with France's {{vat-rate}}% VAT, in US dollars.

| Accounts                                           | A month, per account   |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| {{accounts-band-1-from}} to {{accounts-band-1-to}} | {{accounts-band-1-en}} |
| {{accounts-band-2-from}} to {{accounts-band-2-to}} | {{accounts-band-2-en}} |
| {{accounts-band-3-from}} to {{accounts-band-3-to}} | {{accounts-band-3-en}} |
| {{accounts-band-4-from}} and up                    | {{accounts-band-4-en}} |

### How accounts are counted [#counting]

Accounts are counted by the day:

- Each UTC day of the month counts the most accounts connected at once that day, across the billing account. Only days 1 to {{days-per-month}} count, so day {{day-not-counted}} is never counted.
- At the end of the month, the account-days are added up and divided by {{days-per-month}}. That number of accounts, which may hold a fraction, goes through the ladder, and the first {{free-account-days}} account-days are free.
- An account you disconnect stops counting from the next day. Nothing else is charged for accounts: there's no fee of any kind.
- A channel that's turned off or needs reconnecting still counts. An ad account doesn't.
- A chat counts as one account: a Discord channel, a Telegram channel or group, or a Slack channel.
- On the first of each month, the invoice bills the month just past.

With every account connected all month, a month costs:

| {{accounts-10-count-en}}   | {{accounts-10-en}}   |

### VAT and currency [#vat]

Prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT. The VAT you pay depends on your country. Customers in the EU have it added at checkout, at their country's rate. A business with a valid VAT number pays the price excluding VAT, because the VAT is reverse-charged. In France, French VAT applies.

At checkout you can pay in your own currency, converted by our payment provider at the day's rate. Otherwise your card is charged in US dollars. Invoices are in US dollars.

The workspace's **Billing** page shows its plan and how much of each limit it uses. To open it, click **Settings** at the foot of the workspace's sidebar, then **Billing**, under **Administration**. `GET /api/v1/workspace/billing` answers the same.

## Usage meters [#meters]

Whatever costs Peal by use has a meter. Each meter has a monthly allowance per billing account, across all its workspaces, from the first of each calendar month (UTC):

| Meter                                                                          | Included each month       | Past the allowance                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Messages sent from the [inbox](/guides/engage/inbox), comment replies included | {{messages-allowance-en}} | {{messages-price-en}} for {{messages-per-en}}, billed by the message |
| [Managed ads](/guides/engage/ads), each ad active in the month                 | {{ads-allowance-en}}      | {{ads-price-en}} per ad                                              |
| The writing assistant's requests and images, on the instance's models          | coming soon               | coming soon                                                          |
| [X](/platforms/x)'s API: posts sent and, where turned on, counts read          | nothing                   | at X's rates ([below](#x))                                           |

What happens at the allowance depends on the payment method:

- Without one, a meter pauses at its allowance until the first of the next month. Reading, analytics and publishing go on.
- With one, each unit past the allowance is billed on the month's invoice.
- While a payment has failed, the allowances still apply, and nothing past them is used until the payment goes through.

Until a billing account's first invoice is paid, what its meters bill is capped, whatever the ceilings, and that includes the months since its subscription began.

Past what a meter allows, the action is refused and nothing is counted. The API answers 402 `UsageAllowanceReached`, with the `meter`, what it `allowed`, what was `used`, when it `resetsAt` and the `reason` ([Errors](/developers/conventions/errors)).

### The writing assistant [#assistant-meter]

The writing assistant on the instance's models needs a payment method. With one, its requests and its images each have a monthly allowance per billing account, which the billing page shows. Until their prices are set, nothing past the allowance is billed, and the assistant stops there until the next month.

A generation that fails once the provider had it isn't counted, up to the allowance. A workspace's own [image](/guides/workspace#providers) and [text](/guides/workspace#providers) providers cost nothing, on any plan.

### X, at X's rates [#x]

X bills each call to its API, and Peal passes that on at X's published pay-per-use rates, with nothing added. A billing account needs a payment method before it connects an X account. X's use is billed on its monthly invoice, up to a ceiling of {{x-ceiling-en}} excluding tax a month, which its billing managers can change on the billing page.

| A call                                      | X's rate           |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| A post sent, each part of a thread          | {{x-post-en}}      |
| A post sent with a link                     | {{x-post-link-en}} |
| A post's counts read, once a UTC day        | {{x-post-read-en}} |
| An account's followers read, once a UTC day | {{x-user-read-en}} |

The rates are those X's developer pricing page gave on {{x-rates-read-en}}. X may change them, and the billing page shows the ones billed. Reads X doesn't return (a refusal, a deleted post) aren't billed.

Reading counts is off until a member who manages billing turns it on for the workspace, on the billing page ([Analytics](/guides/engage/analytics)).

#### At the ceiling [#x-ceiling]

Before each part of a thread goes out, Peal checks that the meter has room for every part still to go. A thread with no room from its first part isn't sent at all.

At the ceiling, posts to X fail without being sent (`xCeilingReached`), and counts wait for the next month. Every other platform goes on. Billing managers are told at 80% and at 100% of the ceiling.

#### When an X post fails [#x-failures]

An X post also fails, without being sent, when the billing account:

- has no payment method (`paymentMethodRequired`);
- had its last payment fail (`paymentFailed`);
- is on a plan from before per-account pricing (`perAccountPricingRequired`).

A thread can also be cut part way, with its first parts live. It then fails as `xThreadCut`. That happens because the check isn't a hold: room the check found was taken before a later part by another claim (another post or X channel of the account, or X's reads), or the account changed meanwhile.

A server you run yourself bills nothing for X. Its operator's settings decide ([Environment variables](/self-hosting/environment-variables)).

## A payment method [#payment-method]

Ordering per-account pricing ([Ordering](#ordering)) adds a payment method to the billing account and moves it off the free tier. Here's what changes:

| What                                                                                                  | Without a payment method                       | With a payment method                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Connected accounts                                                                                    | The first {{free-accounts}}, and no more       | Any number, billed by the ladder                                                  |
| Meters                                                                                                | Each stops at its allowance                    | Use past the allowance is billed                                                  |
| Writing assistant, on the instance's models                                                           | None                                           | A monthly allowance                                                               |
| Media storage (fair use)                                                                              | {{free-storage-en}} across the billing account | {{storage-per-account-en}} per connected account, {{storage-minimum-en}} at least |
| [API requests](/developers/conventions/rate-limits#rate-limits) a minute, per key and per application | {{api-without-en}}                             | {{api-with-en}}, or {{api-on-request-en}} on request                              |

A billing account can make as many workspaces as it likes. They share one ladder, one set of free accounts and one allowance of each meter, so another workspace adds nothing free.

The free tier is one per person. A person pays for one billing account at most, and staff can merge billing accounts opened to multiply it, or require a payment method of one.

## Billing accounts [#accounts]

Every workspace belongs to one billing account, which pays for it:

- The person who creates a workspace pays for it. Each workspace they create joins their billing account, which was made with their first one.
- A workspace made before billing accounts is on its oldest owner's.
- A [partner account](/developers/embedding)'s home workspace and the workspaces it owns are on the partner's billing account, apart from its owners' own.

The workspaces of an account share one plan and count their channels together. A free account holds the free tier's {{free-accounts}} accounts across all its workspaces.

### The billing workspace [#billing-workspace]

The plan is ordered, cancelled and withdrawn from on the account's billing workspace, which holds the subscription. That's its oldest workspace, or a partner's home workspace.

The **Billing** page of every other workspace of the account names the billing workspace, with a link for its members, and orders nothing (409 `billed_elsewhere`). Those workspaces read the account's plan, connected accounts and limits, but none of its money or contract.

A workspace with a plan subscription of its own from before keeps it alone until it ends, then joins its payer's account. On a server that doesn't bill, the accounts are kept and limit nothing.

### Hand a workspace to another owner [#transfer]

The payer of a workspace can hand it to another of its owners, on its **Billing** page. The offer is open for 7 days. The owner it's made to accepts or declines it there, in person. Accepting moves the workspace to their billing account, which is made if they have none.

The handover is refused in two cases:

- when their account's plan has no room for the workspace's channels;
- for the billing workspace while its subscription may still charge. Cancel it first, or hand over another workspace.

The API's `POST …/workspace/billing/transfer`, `POST …/workspace/billing/transfer/acceptance` and `DELETE …/workspace/billing/transfer` answer only a person in their own session.

<Accordion>
<AccordionItem title="When the payer leaves">

On a server that bills, the payer stays one of the workspace's owners until they hand it over. Leaving it, being removed from it or losing the owner role answers 400 `PAYER_MUST_STAY`, and a reduction that would remove them answers `payerRequired`.

On a server that doesn't bill, nothing is refused. A workspace whose payer is no longer one of its owners moves to its oldest owner's account.

When the payer closes their account, each workspace they paid for that others own goes to its oldest other owner's account. It goes there over its plan if that account has no room for its channels.

</AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## What a plan limits [#limits]

A workspace never goes above its plan. Each limit is checked when something is added, requests sent together included, and whatever the plan has no room for is refused:

| Limit             | How it's checked                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Channels          | On the free tier, connecting an account past the first {{free-accounts}} of the billing account is refused. With a payment method, nothing is. An Instagram account is one channel whichever way it's connected: moving it from a Facebook Page to Instagram, or back, adds none, even on a full plan.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Members           | Pending invitations included, when inviting and again when an invitation is accepted. Only on the plans priced by channels that limit them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Posts             | Posts going out in a calendar month (UTC), checked again when each post is due. Only on the plans priced by channels that limit them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Writing assistant | On the free tier and per-account pricing, a [usage meter](#meters): each request and each image counts once against the billing account's month, and none are allowed without a payment method. On a plan priced by channels, requests per UTC day, as the plan gives them, with an image of the instance's model costing 5 of them ([Generate an image](/guides/media#generate)). An image from the workspace's own [image provider](/guides/workspace#providers), and a suggestion from its own [text provider](/guides/workspace#providers), cost none, on any plan, and are never metered. |
| Media             | The bytes of images and videos stored, uploads in progress included. An upload the plan has no room for is refused before anything is sent, so delete media to make room. The room an upload is given is held for it for six hours. If it finishes later, it needs room again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

### When a limit is reached [#refusals]

In the web app, a refusal opens a dialog that says which limit was reached, with what the workspace holds and its plan. It offers per-account pricing, with what the next account costs:

- Owners and admins can add a payment method there, by card, as on the **Billing** page ([Ordering](#ordering)), and then go back to what they were doing.
- Other members see who can.

Over the API, each refusal answers 402 `PlanLimitReached`. It carries the `limit`, what the plan `allowed`, what the workspace holds (`used`), its `plan`, the plan with room and the billing page (`billingUrl`). The plan with room (`planWithRoom`) is `payAsYouGo` when a payment method gives room, and null when no plan holds that much.

The writing assistant's daily allowance answers 429 `AssistantQuotaExceeded`, with the same plan fields. The command line and MCP tools say the same in words, with the plan to order and the billing page.

## A workspace over its plan [#over]

A plan can shrink under a workspace. Its trial ends, or its subscription is cancelled or left unpaid, and it's back on the free tier. Nothing is deleted, so the workspace may then hold more than its plan allows.

If it has more channels than the plan allows across its billing account (or more members, on a plan priced by channels that limits them), it has to come back within its plan before it does anything else:

- Every member who opens the workspace sees what it holds beyond the plan.
- Owners and admins can add a payment method, which covers everything. Or they choose which channels stay connected (and, on such a plan, which members stay). Other members see who can do this.
- The other channels are disconnected, and the other members removed, in one confirmed step, and pending invitations are revoked. Disconnecting works as it does for each channel by hand: its tokens are deleted, and the platforms that allow it are asked to forget Peal.
- More media than the plan allows refuses uploads only. The library stays open, so you can delete from it.

<Callout
  type="warning"
  title="Nothing is published until the workspace is within its plan"
>
  Until then nothing is scheduled or published. Posts already scheduled are kept
  as they are, and fail on their channels when due, with `planLimit` as the
  reason and nothing sent. Once the workspace is within its plan, post each one
  again from its page.
</Callout>

Those posts aren't moved back to drafts. A failed post keeps its time, text and channels, tells its author by notice and email, and can be posted again in one step. A draft would lose its schedule without telling anyone.

### When Peal Cloud starts billing [#billing-start]

A workspace on the free tier with more accounts than it includes keeps scheduling and publishing on the accounts it had then, until a day Peal announces. It connects no more meanwhile. An account connected since isn't covered, and a workspace whose subscription ends meanwhile keeps the accounts it had then, until that day. Its billing managers are told, in the app and by email, and every member sees the day.

### What the workspace holds beyond its plan [#standing]

Three days before a trial ends, the reminder lists what the workspace holds beyond the free tier.

`GET /api/v1/workspace/billing/standing` answers the same for every member. It gives what's over, whether the workspace has to come back within its plan, until when a grace keeps it publishing (`graceUntil`), and the plan that holds everything.

## Usage [#usage]

Under the plan, the workspace's **Billing** page shows what it did day by day over the last 7, 30 or 90 days. That's the posts that went out and the channels it had connected, as a chart and as a table, with the period's totals.

You can get the same from `GET /api/v1/workspace/usage?from=2026-10-01&to=2026-10-31`, [`peal usage`](/developers/cli/workspace#usage) or the MCP tool [`get_usage`](/developers/mcp). They answer whoever reads the workspace's billing: owners and admins, an API key or an application given it, and a partner's keys in the workspaces the partner owns.

They're counts only, and nothing is priced, charged or limited by them. A server that doesn't bill keeps and answers the same counts, with `plan` and `allowances` null.

<Accordion>
<AccordionItem title="How usage is counted">

- Days are UTC days, as for every limit. `from` and `to` are both included, 92 days at most. Without them, you get the 30 days that end today. A range that ends before it starts, or is longer, answers 400 `invalid_date_range`.
- Each day counts the posts that went out on it, one for each channel a post went out on, by platform. A thread counts once.
- A post sent again after it failed counts again, because it's another post on the platform. A post whose outcome couldn't be confirmed isn't counted, even once someone confirms that it went out.
- The day also gives the workspace's requests to the writing assistant, which the daily note below copies. It's `null` for a day without the note, today included, until the note is taken.
- Shortly after each UTC midnight (at 00:11, or up to 12 hours later after an outage), the server notes what every workspace holds then, for the day that just ended: the channels it connected itself, its members and the bytes of media it stores.
- A day without that note (before usage was counted, or a day the note couldn't be taken) answers `null`, and shows a dash on the page, never another day's figure.
- The totals add up the period's posts, by platform, and its channel-days. Channel-days are each day's channels added up, leaving out the days without a note, with how many days they cover (`daysCounted`).
- For each calendar month the period touches, the totals also give the posts on platforms that bill the server for each post (X), with how many held a link. These count each part of a thread, as X bills each: a thread of three parts is one of the day's posts, and three here.
- `current` is what each limit counts now, as on the **Billing** page. A workspace a partner owns counts its channels against the partner's plan, and `sharedWith` names the partner's workspace.
- The counts are kept {{keep-usage-en}}, then deleted. A range that starts earlier answers what's kept. They're deleted with the workspace, and its data export holds them.
- A partner account reads the same across every workspace it holds, per customer: [Usage per customer](/developers/embedding/operate#usage).

</AccordionItem>

</Accordion>

## Ordering [#ordering]

Owners and admins order per-account pricing on the billing workspace's **Billing** page, in their own session.

<Steps>
<Step title="Tick the box">

Before anything is paid, tick a box that's never ticked for you: "I ask Peal to start per-account pricing now: each connected account past the free ones, counted by the day, and each use past its monthly allowance are billed at the rate card’s prices, excluding tax, each month for the month past. I know that, as a consumer, I may withdraw within 14 days, with “Withdraw from contract here”, on this page, and then pay only for what was used." Peal records it with the order.

</Step>
<Step title="Pay at Stripe">

Click **Order with obligation to pay** to open Stripe's checkout. Stripe asks for a card, though nothing is due yet, the billing address and, for a business, the VAT number, and it computes the VAT.

</Step>
<Step title="Get the confirmation">

A confirmation comes by email, with the rates and the contract number. The contract number lets you cancel or withdraw without signing in.

</Step>
</Steps>

Once it's ordered:

- The subscription bills calendar months (UTC), from the first of the next month.
- Stripe's portal changes the payment method and gives the invoices and receipts, also once the plan has ended (**Invoices and receipts**).

Some orders are refused:

- Only per-account pricing is ordered, monthly. Another plan or a year is refused (409 `PlanNotSold`).
- An account that already has it, even past due or unpaid, orders nothing more (409 `AlreadySubscribed`). To settle a failed payment, update the payment method in Stripe's portal.
- An order from a page whose box shows older words, or names none, is refused (409 `ConsentChanged`). Load the page again.

A workspace on a plan priced by channels, or in a trial, can order it too. Once per-account pricing starts, that plan or trial ends at once, and the plan's unused time is refunded to the payment method it was paid with ([Move to per-account pricing](#legacy-move)).

## Cancelling [#cancelling]

You can cancel online at any time with **Cancel your contract**. The link is on the **Billing** page.

The page asks only for what identifies you and the contract: your name, the email address the acknowledgement goes to, the contract, and, if you like, the day you want it to end. It then shows a summary to check, and **Notify the cancellation** sends it.

How it's applied depends on how you identify the contract:

| How you cancel                                                                                              | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Signed in, as an owner or an admin                                                                          | Your name and address are filled in, you pick the contract from your workspaces, and the cancellation applies at once. The **Billing** page has the same two steps for its own workspace.                                                                                                                                                           |
| With the contract number, which is in the email that confirmed the subscription and in the renewal reminder | Applied at once, without signing in, whatever address you give.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| With neither                                                                                                | Peal emails the address an acknowledgement. If that address manages a workspace's billing, the email holds a link, good for 30 days, that shows its contracts and applies the cancellation to the one you choose. Otherwise a person looks at the request and writes to you. That way, someone who only knows your address can't end your contract. |

Whichever way you cancel, the cancellation counts from the moment Peal received it, which the acknowledgement states. A renewal charged after that moment is refunded.

When it ends depends on the plan:

- Per-account pricing ends with the month, whose use the last invoice bills.
- A plan priced by channels ends with the period paid for, and nothing more is charged.
- With a later day, it ends on that day, and the last period is charged only up to it.
- A trial ends at once.

The members who manage billing get the confirmation by email, with the date it takes effect. Deleting the workspace cancels at once too ([Workspaces and your account](/guides/workspace#delete-workspace)).

## Withdrawing within 14 days [#withdrawal]

As a consumer, you can withdraw from the contract within 14 days of subscribing, without giving a reason. Use **Withdraw from contract here**, on the **Billing** page while the 14 days run, even after cancelling.

It works as cancelling does. You give your name, the address for the acknowledgement and the contract, check a summary, then click **Confirm withdrawal**. It applies at once when you're signed in or give the contract number. Otherwise it applies from the link Peal emails you.

The 14 days and the refund count from the moment Peal received your statement. The acknowledgement states that moment, with what you sent.

Withdrawing ends the subscription at once. For each payment since you subscribed, the unused part of the period it paid for is refunded, to the second, to your payment method within 14 days, through a credit note on each invoice. Per-account pricing is billed for the month past, so withdrawing invoices its use up to that moment, and nothing paid ahead is refunded.

  type="info"
  title="A business with a verified VAT number can't withdraw"
>
  A business that gave a VAT number Stripe verified has no right to withdraw,
  and can cancel instead.
</Callout>

## Plans priced by channels [#legacy-plans]

These plans are no longer sold. A subscription to one keeps it, with its price and the limits below, until it ends. Such a plan is paid in advance and holds its workspace alone, apart from its billing account, until it ends. The workspace then joins its billing account's plan.

### Move to per-account pricing [#legacy-move]

Under **Move to per-account pricing**, on the **Billing** page, you order per-account pricing as adding a payment method does ([Ordering](#ordering)), with its own checkout.

Once per-account pricing starts, the plan ends at once. The unused part of the period it paid for goes back to the payment method it was paid with, to the second, through a credit note on each invoice. A trial ends the same way, with nothing to refund.

### Retirement [#legacy-retirement]

These plans are being retired. Each subscription is told the day it ends, by notice and email to the members who manage billing. That day is the first midnight UTC at least 30 days after the notice.

Nothing after that day is charged, whatever the plan's period. What was paid for beyond it is refunded to the payment method it was paid with, through a credit note on each invoice. A period that renews before that day is charged only up to it. The workspace is then on the free tier, unless it moved before.

## Trials [#trial]

No trial is offered any more, because the free accounts replace it. A trial started before runs to its end, without a charge, and the workspace is then on the free tier, with its content. Three days before, the members who manage billing get a reminder, with what the workspace holds beyond the free tier.

## Yearly plans [#renewal]

Yearly plans are no longer sold. A yearly plan priced by channels renews each year until it's cancelled.

45 days before it renews, and never less than a month before, the members who manage billing get a reminder, in the app and by an email of its own. It says the contract doesn't have to be renewed, and gives:

- what Stripe will charge, VAT included;
- the last day to refuse the renewal, in a box;
- the contract number;
- how to cancel.

A price change is announced by email 30 days ahead, and applies from the next renewal.
