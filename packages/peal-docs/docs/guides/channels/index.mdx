A channel is a social account connected to a workspace. Posts go to channels, so connect at least one before you schedule anything.

Open a workspace from **Workspaces**, then **Channels**. Every member sees the workspace's channels. Only owners and admins connect, reconnect and disconnect them.

<Callout type="note" title="Eleven platforms so far">
  Peal connects Mastodon, Bluesky, LinkedIn, X, Threads, Instagram, TikTok and
  Pinterest accounts, Facebook Pages, YouTube channels and Google Business
  Profile locations. **Channels** offers only the platforms the Peal server is
  set up for.
</Callout>

A WordPress site connects too, with an application password, to publish articles: see [Websites and articles](/guides/channels/websites).

## Connect an account [#connect]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels">
    Under the platform, choose its **Connect** button, such as **Connect
    Bluesky**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in on the platform">
    Sign in, and agree to what Peal asks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back to Peal">
    The platform sends you back to Peal, which finishes the connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Finish in the same browser tab you started in, within 15 minutes.
</Callout>

Connecting an account the workspace already has reconnects its channel instead of adding a second one. Several workspaces may connect the same account.

Each platform's page says how to connect an account there: [Mastodon](/platforms/mastodon#connect), [Bluesky](/platforms/bluesky#connect), [Threads](/platforms/threads#connect), [X](/platforms/x#connect), [LinkedIn](/platforms/linkedin#connect), [Facebook Pages](/platforms/facebook#connect), [Instagram](/platforms/instagram#connect), [TikTok](/platforms/tiktok#connect), [YouTube](/platforms/youtube#connect), [Pinterest](/platforms/pinterest#connect), [Google Business Profile](/platforms/google-business#connect).

## Let someone else connect their account [#connect-links]

The account to connect may belong to a client, a colleague or a customer. A **connect link** opens a hosted page where the account's owner signs in on the platform and connects the account to the workspace. They need no Peal account, and they never see the workspace.

Members who may connect channels, and API keys with `channel:connect`, create a link for one platform:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="CLI">

```sh
peal links create --platform linkedin
```

See [`peal links create`](/developers/cli/workspace#links).

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">

```http
POST /api/workspaces/{workspaceId}/connect-links
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="MCP">
    The [MCP](/developers/mcp/tools#tools) tool `create_connect_link`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The link's address is shown once. Hand it over a channel you trust.

| Question                | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How many times it works | Once. Opening it uses nothing up; a sign-in that connects an account does.                                                                                                                         |
| How long it works       | A week unless you say otherwise (1 hour to 30 days). You can revoke it.                                                                                                                            |
| What its holder can do  | Connect an account of theirs to the workspace while the link waits, and nothing else: it reads no channel and no post.                                                                             |
| Reconnecting a channel  | With `channelId`, the link reconnects that channel, and only a sign-in to the same account completes.                                                                                              |
| What the page shows     | It is in English or French, and names the workspace's [partner account](/developers/embedding) when it has one, the workspace otherwise.                                                           |
| How to follow it        | Its `status` (`pending`, `completed` with the channels it connected, `revoked` or `expired`), and [`channel.connected`](/developers/webhooks/events/channels#channel-connected) tells your server. |

[Embedding Peal](/developers/embedding#connect) tells how a product sends its own customers through such a page and back.

## Statuses [#statuses]

Each channel shows one of these:

| Status           | What it means                                                                                                                |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Connected        | Peal can post to the account.                                                                                                |
| Reconnect needed | The platform no longer accepts Peal's access. Nothing goes out on the channel until you reconnect it.                        |
| Turned off       | Nothing goes out on the channel, and reconnecting leaves it off. To post to the account again, [turn it back on](#turn-off). |

A channel whose access cannot be renewed also shows the date by which it must be reconnected. Its workspace's owners and admins are told ahead of that date.

## Keep channels connected [#keep-connected]

Peal renews each account's access on its own before it runs out:

| Platform                                                        | How Peal keeps the access                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bluesky                                                         | Refreshes the session a month before it would lapse.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Threads, and Instagram accounts connected with Instagram        | The access lasts 60 days. Peal renews it a week before.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Pinterest                                                       | The access lasts 30 days. Peal renews it a week before, and Pinterest lets it do so for as long as it keeps renewing, each renewal within 60 days of the last.                                                                                                          |
| X                                                               | The access lasts two hours. Peal renews it whenever it posts or reads the posts' counts, and at least once a month for an account it does nothing with, so the platform never drops it.                                                                                 |
| TikTok                                                          | The access lasts a day or less, and is renewed like X's. TikTok lets Peal renew it for 180 days after you connected the account: then the channel asks to be reconnected.                                                                                               |
| YouTube                                                         | The access lasts an hour, and is renewed like X's. Google ends it when the owner removes Peal from their Google account, or six months after Peal last used it; on a self-hosted server whose Google project is in testing, seven days after the channel was connected. |
| Google Business Profile                                         | The access lasts an hour, and is renewed like X's.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Mastodon                                                        | The access does not expire until someone revokes it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Facebook Pages, and Instagram accounts connected through a Page | They post with Page access, which does not expire until someone revokes it. Facebook also withdraws it when the person who connected the Page changes their password or loses their role on the Page.                                                                   |
| LinkedIn                                                        | The access cannot be renewed: reconnect the channel every 60 days, by the date it shows.                                                                                                                                                                                |

When someone removes Peal from their Facebook, Instagram or Threads account, Meta tells Peal. Peal then marks the channels that person connected to be reconnected, in every workspace, and tells the members who manage them, before a post fails on them.

### When a platform refuses the access [#refused]

When a platform refuses Peal's access while Peal posts, Peal renews the access where the platform allows it, and tries once more.

If the platform refuses again, or the access cannot be renewed (for example because the account's owner revoked it), the channel turns to **Reconnect needed**. The workspace's owners and admins are told once, under the bell and by email unless they turned that email off (see [Notifications](/guides/workspace/notifications)).

When a platform does not answer, Peal tries again later and leaves the channel as it is.

## What Peal posts [#posting]

At a post's time, Peal posts it as the channel's account, with its text on that channel and its images and videos, each with its alt text when it has one. [Images and videos](/guides/media#platforms) lists what each platform accepts. How each platform takes the post:

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="Mastodon">
    Peal posts on the account's server, with the account's default visibility. The server turns mentions, hashtags and links into links itself, and may process a video before the post can show it.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Bluesky">
    Peal posts on the server that holds the account. Links and hashtags become links; mentions stay plain text for now. Videos go through Bluesky's video service, which processes them before the post goes out.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="LinkedIn">
    Peal posts as the member, or as the company page, publicly unless the post's options say otherwise (a profile's connections only, or signed-in members). Hashtags become LinkedIn hashtags.

    A company page the text names after an `@`, by its full name exactly as LinkedIn shows it, becomes a mention when the post's options list the page. A company page channel finds a page by the name in its address. People cannot be mentioned, and any other `@` stays plain text.

    A link can show as a card, with the title and description you give it, as LinkedIn reads no page itself. A PDF shows page by page, alone, under the title you give it. LinkedIn processes a video or a document before the post goes out.

  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="X">
    Peal posts as the account. X turns mentions, hashtags and links into links itself, a domain without `https://` such as `peal.social` too, and processes a video before the post goes out.

    When X refuses the server's app because its credits ran out, the post is not at fault. Peal keeps it waiting and tries again every half hour, so it goes out once the server's operator has paid. A day after its time, it fails with its reason (see [Notifications](/guides/workspace/notifications)).

  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Threads and Instagram">
    Meta fetches each image and video from the media store and processes it, then Peal publishes the post. Several files make a carousel. An Instagram video goes out as a reel. Peal checks the account's daily publishing limit before it starts.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Facebook Pages">
    Peal posts the text on the Page, a photo with it, several photos in one post, or a video, which Facebook fetches from the media store.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="TikTok">
    Peal posts one video, with the post's text as its caption, or up to 35 photos, with the text as their description, as you chose in the post's [TikTok options](/guides/posts#options). Just before, it reads the account's settings on TikTok again, and the post fails if they no longer allow it.

    A video goes up in parts from the media store, unless TikTok can fetch it itself (see [TikTok](/self-hosting/platforms#tiktok)). TikTok always fetches photos.

    TikTok then processes the post, which Peal follows until it is published. A post others see shows a link to it once TikTok's moderation passed it; one only you see has none. TikTok allows about 15 posts a day to each account, shared by every app.

  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="YouTube">
    Peal uploads the post's video to the channel, with the post's text as its description and the title, visibility, made-for-kids answer, tags and category of its [platform options](/guides/posts#options), then waits while YouTube processes it.

    A video YouTube fails to process or rejects, as a duplicate or for its length, fails with YouTube's reason. When YouTube keeps a video private although you chose public or unlisted, the post's page says so: change its visibility in YouTube Studio.

    A Peal server uploads a limited number of videos a day, across all its channels. Past that, a video waits until midnight Pacific Time, when YouTube's quotas reset, and the post's page shows the wait.

  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Google Business Profile">
    Peal makes a post on the location, with the post's text as its description and the type, dates, button and offer of its [platform options](/guides/posts#options). Google fetches each photo from the media store; a post takes no video.

    Google may reject a post for its content policy after taking it: the post then fails. Once the post is live, its page links to it on Google.

  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Pinterest">
    Peal creates a Pin on the board, and the board section, of the post's [platform options](/guides/posts#options), with the post's text as its description, the first file's alt text as its alt text, and the title and link you gave.

    One image makes an image Pin, two to five a carousel, which Pinterest fetches from the media store. A video goes up from the media store in one request and Pinterest processes it. Its cover is the frame at the second you chose, the first one otherwise.

    Pinterest gives Peal nothing to tell a Pin it already made from one it did not. When its answer is lost, Peal looks for the Pin on the board, and never sends it twice.

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Name a channel [#name]

Two accounts on the same platform can look alike in a list. Choose **Rename** next to a channel to give it a name of your own, of 60 characters at most, such as "Client A" or "Support".

The name is Peal's alone. It shows beside the handle and the name the platform gives the account, which stay, and it is sent to no platform. Clear it to take it back.

## Turn a channel off [#turn-off]

Choose **Turn off** next to a channel to stop posting to an account without disconnecting it. The channel keeps its connection, its history and its name. It still counts in your plan, and gets no reconnect reminder meanwhile.

<Callout type="warning">
  A post that reaches its time while one of its channels is off fails on that
  channel, with the reason "Channel turned off", and goes out on its others. It
  is not sent later: turn the channel back on, then schedule it in a new post.
</Callout>

Choose **Turn on** to post to the account again. If the platform stopped accepting Peal's access while the channel was off, it shows **Reconnect needed**: [reconnect](#reconnect) it.

Naming a channel and turning it off or on take the right to connect channels. The same through the command line or the API:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="CLI">

```sh
peal channels list --platform youtube --status disabled --search "client a"
peal channels rename <channel-id> "Client A"
peal channels rename <channel-id> --clear
peal channels disable <channel-id>
peal channels enable <channel-id>
```

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    `PATCH …/channels/{channelId}`, `POST …/channels/{channelId}/disable` and `…/enable`. `GET …/channels/search` lists a workspace's channels a page at a time, by platform, status or text, with how many are in each status.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Reconnect [#reconnect]

Choose **Reconnect** next to the channel, then sign in on the platform with the same account. Signing in with another account is refused: to post to it, connect it as a new channel.

## Disconnect [#disconnect]

Choose **Disconnect** next to the channel and confirm. Peal deletes the tokens it holds for the account and posts nothing more to it. You can connect it again later.

<Callout type="info">
  Disconnecting tells only TikTok, YouTube and Google Business Profile: unless
  another workspace still connects the same account, Peal revokes its access
  there and leaves the account's connected apps. On the other platforms, revoke
  Peal's access in the account's settings there.
</Callout>

## When connecting fails [#errors]

| Message                                                          | What to do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| This server is not set up to connect accounts of this platform   | Ask the instance's administrator: the server lacks a setting the platform needs.                                                                                                                                                           |
| This is not the address of a server                              | Check the Mastodon server's address, for example `mastodon.social`.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Peal only connects to servers on the public internet, over HTTPS | The server is private, or does not answer over HTTPS.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| No account was found for this handle                             | Check the Bluesky handle.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| This connection expired or was started elsewhere                 | Start again from the workspace's channels, in this tab.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| The connection was cancelled on the platform                     | You, or the platform, refused. Start again if you meant to connect.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| You signed in to another account than this channel's             | Reconnect with the channel's own account.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| The sign-in reached no account to connect                        | Manage a Facebook Page, or link your Instagram professional account to one, or connect it with Instagram instead; on YouTube, pick a Google account that has a channel; on Google Business Profile, verify the business with Google first. |
| The platform did not answer                                      | Try again in a moment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
