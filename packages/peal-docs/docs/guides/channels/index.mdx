A channel is a social account connected to a workspace. Posts go to channels, so connect at least one before you schedule anything.

To see a workspace's channels, open the workspace and click **Channels** in the sidebar. Every member sees them. Only owners and admins can connect, reconnect and disconnect them.

<Callout type="note" title="Peal connects fourteen platforms so far">
  Peal connects Mastodon, Bluesky, LinkedIn, X, Threads, Instagram, TikTok and
  Pinterest accounts, Facebook Pages, YouTube channels, Google Business Profile
  locations, and Discord, Telegram and Slack channels. **Channels** offers only
  the platforms the Peal server is set up for.
</Callout>

A WordPress site connects too, with an application password, so you can publish articles to it. See [Websites and articles](/guides/channels/websites).

A Discord or Telegram chat connects without a sign-in page. You paste a secret instead: a Discord channel's webhook address, or your own Telegram bot's token and the chat, for a Telegram channel or group. Peal checks it on the platform and keeps it encrypted. To reconnect the channel, fill in the same form with a new secret. See [Discord](/platforms/discord#connect) and [Telegram](/platforms/telegram#connect).

A Slack channel connects with a sign-in. You add Peal's app to your Slack workspace, then pick the channel on Slack's page. See [Slack](/platforms/slack#connect).

## Connect an account [#connect]

<Callout type="info">
  A new workspace opens on **Set up your channels**: tick the platforms you post
  to and click **Connect**, or **Skip for now**. Peal opens each platform's
  sign-in in turn, on the account the platform names: if you're signed in to
  several, check it there. If one fails, Peal comes back to the step, where
  **Continue** goes on with the next.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick the platform">

On **Channels**, click **New channel**, then the platform. To find it, type its name in **Search platforms**, or pick a type (**Social network**, **Chat**, **Website**). **Filter** shows the platforms that have a feature, such as **Analytics** or **Direct messages**.

</Step>
<Step title="Click the platform's Connect button">

Fill in what the platform asks for, if anything, then click its **Connect** button, such as **Connect Bluesky**.

</Step>
<Step title="Sign in on the platform">

Sign in, and agree to what Peal asks for.

</Step>
<Step title="Come back to Peal">

The platform sends you back to Peal, which finishes the connection.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout
  type="info"
  title="A connection finishes only in its own tab, within 15 minutes"
>
  Peal finishes the connection only in the browser tab you started in, and only
  within 15 minutes.
</Callout>

If the workspace already has the account, connecting it again reconnects its channel instead of adding a second one. Several workspaces can connect the same account.

Each platform's page says how to connect an account there: [Mastodon](/platforms/mastodon#connect), [Bluesky](/platforms/bluesky#connect), [Threads](/platforms/threads#connect), [X](/platforms/x#connect), [LinkedIn](/platforms/linkedin#connect), [Facebook Pages](/platforms/facebook#connect), [Instagram](/platforms/instagram#connect), [TikTok](/platforms/tiktok#connect), [YouTube](/platforms/youtube#connect), [Pinterest](/platforms/pinterest#connect), [Google Business Profile](/platforms/google-business#connect), [Discord](/platforms/discord#connect), [Telegram](/platforms/telegram#connect), [Slack](/platforms/slack#connect).

## Let someone else connect their account [#connect-links]

The account you need may belong to a client, a colleague or a customer. Send them a **connect link**. It opens a hosted page where they sign in on the platform and connect the account to the workspace. They don't need a Peal account, and they never see the workspace.

Members who may connect channels, and API keys with `channel:connect`, can create a link for one platform:

<Tabs>
<Tab title="CLI">

```sh
peal links create --platform linkedin
```

See [`peal links create`](/developers/cli/workspace#links).

</Tab>
<Tab title="API">

```http
POST /api/v1/connect-links
```

</Tab>
<Tab title="MCP">

The [MCP](/developers/mcp/tools#tools) tool `create_connect_link`.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

The link's address is shown once. Hand it over a channel you trust.

Here's how a link behaves:

| Question                | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How many times it works | Once. Opening it uses nothing up, but a sign-in that connects an account does.                                                                                                                     |
| How long it works       | A week, unless you set otherwise (1 hour to 30 days). You can revoke it.                                                                                                                           |
| What its holder can do  | Connect an account of theirs to the workspace while the link waits, and nothing else. It reads no channel and no post.                                                                             |
| Reconnecting a channel  | With `channelId`, the link reconnects that channel, and only a sign-in to the same account completes it.                                                                                           |
| What the page shows     | It's in English or French, and names the workspace's [partner account](/developers/embedding) if it has one, or the workspace otherwise.                                                           |
| How to follow it        | Its `status` (`pending`, `completed` with the channels it connected, `revoked` or `expired`), and [`channel.connected`](/developers/webhooks/events/channels#channel-connected) tells your server. |

[Embedding Peal](/developers/embedding#connect) shows how a product sends its own customers through such a page and back.

## Statuses [#statuses]

Each channel shows one of these statuses:

| Status           | What it means                                                                                                                |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Connected        | Peal can post to the account.                                                                                                |
| Reconnect needed | The platform no longer accepts Peal's access. Nothing goes out on the channel until you reconnect it.                        |
| Turned off       | Nothing goes out on the channel, and reconnecting leaves it off. To post to the account again, [turn it back on](#turn-off). |

A channel whose access can't be renewed also shows the date by which it must be reconnected. The workspace's owners and admins are told ahead of that date.

## Keep channels connected [#keep-connected]

Peal renews each account's access by itself before it runs out. Here's how it works on each platform:

| Platform                                                        | How Peal keeps the access                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bluesky                                                         | Refreshes the session a month before it would lapse.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Threads, and Instagram accounts connected with Instagram        | The access lasts 60 days. Peal renews it a week before.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Pinterest                                                       | The access lasts 30 days. Peal renews it a week before. Pinterest lets it keep renewing for as long as each renewal comes within 60 days of the last.                                                                                                                           |
| X                                                               | The access lasts two hours. Peal renews it whenever it posts or reads the posts' counts, and at least once a month for an account it does nothing with, so the platform never drops it.                                                                                         |
| TikTok                                                          | The access lasts a day or less, and is renewed like X's. TikTok lets Peal renew it for 180 days after you connected the account. After that, the channel asks to be reconnected.                                                                                                |
| YouTube                                                         | The access lasts an hour, and is renewed like X's. Google ends it when the owner removes Peal from their Google account, or six months after Peal last used it. On a self-hosted server whose Google project is in testing, it ends seven days after the channel was connected. |
| Google Business Profile                                         | The access lasts an hour, and is renewed like X's.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Mastodon                                                        | The access doesn't expire until someone revokes it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Facebook Pages, and Instagram accounts connected through a Page | They post with Page access, which doesn't expire until someone revokes it. Facebook also withdraws it when the person who connected the Page changes their password or loses their role on the Page.                                                                            |
| LinkedIn                                                        | The access can't be renewed. Reconnect the channel every 60 days, by the date it shows.                                                                                                                                                                                         |

When someone removes Peal from their Facebook, Instagram or Threads account, Meta tells Peal. Peal then marks the channels that person connected as needing reconnection, in every workspace, and tells the members who manage them, before a post fails there.

### When a platform refuses the access [#refused]

If a platform refuses Peal's access while Peal is posting, Peal renews the access where the platform allows it, and tries once more.

If the platform refuses again, or the access can't be renewed (because the account's owner revoked it, for example), the channel turns to **Reconnect needed**. The workspace's owners and admins are told once, under the bell and by email, unless they turned that email off (see [Notifications](/guides/workspace/notifications)).

When a platform doesn't answer, Peal tries again later and leaves the channel as it is.

## What Peal posts [#posting]

At a post's time, Peal posts it as the channel's account. It sends the post's text on that channel and its images and videos, each with its alt text when it has one. [Images and videos](/guides/media#platforms) lists what each platform accepts.

Each platform takes the post in its own way:

<Accordion>
<AccordionItem title="Mastodon">

Peal posts on the account's server, with the account's default visibility. The server turns mentions, hashtags and links into links itself, and may process a video before the post can show it.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem title="Bluesky">

Peal posts on the server that holds the account. Links and hashtags become links, and mentions stay plain text for now. Videos go through Bluesky's video service, which processes them before the post goes out.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem title="LinkedIn">

Peal posts as the member, or as the company page. The post is public unless its options say otherwise (a profile's connections only, or signed-in members). Hashtags become LinkedIn hashtags.

A company page becomes a mention when the text names it after an `@`, by its full name exactly as LinkedIn shows it, and the post's options list the page. A company page channel finds a page by the name in its address. People can't be mentioned, and any other `@` stays plain text.

A link can show as a card, with the title and description you give it, because LinkedIn reads no page itself. A PDF shows page by page, on its own, under the title you give it. LinkedIn processes a video or a document before the post goes out.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem title="X">

Peal posts as the account. X turns mentions, hashtags and links into links itself, including a domain without `https://` such as `peal.social`, and processes a video before the post goes out.

When X refuses the server's app because its credits ran out, the post isn't at fault. Peal keeps it waiting and tries again every half hour, so it goes out once the server's operator has paid. A day after its time, it fails with its reason (see [Notifications](/guides/workspace/notifications)).

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem title="Threads and Instagram">

Meta fetches each image and video from the media store and processes it, then Peal publishes the post. Several files make a carousel. An Instagram video goes out as a reel. Peal checks the account's daily publishing limit before it starts.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem title="Facebook Pages">

Peal posts the text on the Page, with a photo, several photos in one post, or a video, which Facebook fetches from the media store.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem title="TikTok">

Peal posts one video, with the post's text as its caption, or up to 35 photos, with the text as their description, as you chose in the post's [TikTok options](/guides/posts#options). Just before, it reads the account's settings on TikTok again, and the post fails if they no longer allow it.

A video goes up in parts from the media store, unless TikTok can fetch it itself (see [TikTok](/self-hosting/platforms#tiktok)). TikTok always fetches photos.

TikTok then processes the post, and Peal follows it until it's published. A post others can see shows a link to it once TikTok's moderation has passed it. A post only you can see has none. TikTok allows about 15 posts a day to each account, shared by every app.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem title="YouTube">

Peal uploads the post's video to the channel, then waits while YouTube processes it. The post's text becomes the description, and the title, visibility, made-for-kids answer, tags and category come from its [platform options](/guides/posts#options).

A video YouTube fails to process or rejects, as a duplicate or for its length, fails with YouTube's reason. When YouTube keeps a video private although you chose public or unlisted, the post's page says so. Change its visibility in YouTube Studio.

A Peal server uploads a limited number of videos a day, across all its channels. Past that, a video waits until midnight Pacific Time, when YouTube's quotas reset, and the post's page shows the wait.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem title="Google Business Profile">

Peal makes a post on the location. The post's text becomes its description, and the type, dates, button and offer come from its [platform options](/guides/posts#options). Google fetches each photo from the media store, and a post takes no video.

Google may reject a post for its content policy after taking it, and the post then fails. Once the post is live, its page links to it on Google.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem title="Pinterest">

Peal creates a Pin on the board, and the board section, set in the post's [platform options](/guides/posts#options). The Pin gets the post's text as its description, the first file's alt text as its alt text, and the title and link you gave.

One image makes an image Pin, and two to five make a carousel, which Pinterest fetches from the media store. A video goes up from the media store in one request, and Pinterest processes it. Its cover is the frame at the second you chose, or the first one otherwise.

Pinterest gives Peal no way to tell a Pin it already made from one it didn't. When its answer is lost, Peal looks for the Pin on the board, and never sends it twice.

</AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Name a channel [#name]

Two accounts on the same platform can look alike in a list. Click **Rename** next to a channel to give it a name of your own, such as "Client A" or "Support". It can be 60 characters at most.

The name is Peal's alone. It shows beside the handle and the name the platform gives the account, which both stay, and it's sent to no platform. To take it back, clear it or click **Remove the name**.

## Turn a channel off [#turn-off]

To stop posting to an account without disconnecting it, click **Turn off** next to its channel. The channel keeps its connection, its history and its name. It still counts in your plan, and gets no reconnect reminder while it's off.

<Callout type="warning" title="A post due on a channel that's off fails there">
  A post that reaches its time while one of its channels is off fails on that
  channel, with the reason "Channel turned off", and goes out on its others. It
  isn't sent later. Turn the channel back on, then schedule it in a new post.
</Callout>

Click **Turn on** to post to the account again. If the platform stopped accepting Peal's access while the channel was off, it shows **Reconnect needed**, so [reconnect](#reconnect) it.

Naming a channel and turning it off or on need the right to connect channels. You can do the same on the command line or through the API:

<Tabs>
<Tab title="CLI">

```sh
peal channels list --platform youtube --status disabled --search "client a"
peal channels rename <channel-id> "Client A"
peal channels rename <channel-id> --clear
peal channels disable <channel-id>
peal channels enable <channel-id>
```

</Tab>
<Tab title="API">

`PATCH …/channels/{channelId}`, `POST …/channels/{channelId}/disable` and `…/enable`. `GET …/channels/search` lists a workspace's channels a page at a time, by platform, status or text, with how many are in each status.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Reconnect [#reconnect]

Click **Reconnect** next to the channel, then sign in on the platform with the same account. Signing in with another account is refused. To post to that other account, connect it as a new channel.

## Disconnect [#disconnect]

Click **Disconnect** next to the channel and confirm. Peal deletes the tokens it holds for the account and posts nothing more to it. You can connect it again later.

<Callout type="info" title="Only TikTok hears about a disconnect">
  Disconnecting tells only TikTok. Unless another workspace still connects the
  same account, Peal revokes its access there and leaves the account's connected
  apps. Google ends access to every YouTube channel and Business Profile
  location of the same Google account at once, so Peal leaves it in place. On
  Google and the other platforms, revoke Peal's access in the account's settings
  there.
</Callout>

## When connecting fails [#errors]

If a connection fails, Peal shows one of these messages:

| Message                                                                | What to do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| This server is not set up to connect accounts of this platform         | Ask the instance's administrator. The server lacks a setting the platform needs.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| This is not the address of a server                                    | Check the Mastodon server's address, for example `mastodon.social`.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Peal only connects to servers on the public internet, over HTTPS       | The server is private, or doesn't answer over HTTPS.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| No account was found for this handle                                   | Check the Bluesky handle.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| This connection expired or was started elsewhere                       | Start again from the workspace's channels, in this tab.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| The connection was cancelled on the platform                           | You, or the platform, refused. Start again if you meant to connect.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| You signed in to another account than this channel's                   | Reconnect with the channel's own account.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| The sign-in reached no account to connect                              | On Facebook, manage a Facebook Page, or link your Instagram professional account to one, or connect it with Instagram instead. On YouTube, pick a Google account that has a channel. On Google Business Profile, verify the business with Google first. |
| The platform did not answer                                            | Try again in a moment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| This is not a Discord webhook's address, and the chats' other refusals | Each one says what to fix. See [Discord](/platforms/discord#connect) and [Telegram](/platforms/telegram#connect).                                                                                                                                       |
