You'll go from a new account to your first scheduled post in the web app. You need an account on one of the [platforms](/platforms) Peal posts to. Each step links to the guide that says more.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a workspace">
    Sign in, or click **Create an account**. Then click **Create a workspace**.
    A workspace holds a team's channels, posts and members, and you can invite
    people to it later ([Workspace settings](/guides/workspace)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel">
    Click **Channels** in the workspace's sidebar, then the platform's
    **Connect** button, such as **Connect Bluesky**. Sign in on the platform and
    allow Peal. The account becomes a channel the workspace posts to. Each
    [platform's page](/platforms) says what it asks for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a post">
    Click **Posts** in the sidebar, then **New post**. Select the channels it
    goes to, and type its text. You'll see each platform's limit and a preview
    per channel as you type, and you can give a channel its own text ([Write a
    post](/guides/posts)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it">
    Under **When**, set the date, time and time zone, then click **Schedule**.
    To send it at once, click **Publish now** instead. The post shows on the
    **Calendar** ([Schedule a post](/guides/posts/schedule)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow it">
    Once the post has gone out, open it from the calendar. For each channel, its
    page shows the status, the post's address on the platform, or why it failed
    ([Follow up on a post](/guides/posts/schedule#follow-up)). The bell tells
    you how it went, and so does an email if you ask for one
    ([Notifications](/guides/workspace/notifications)).
  </Step>
</Steps>
